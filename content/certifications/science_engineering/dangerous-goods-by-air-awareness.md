## Dangerous Goods by Air Awareness

This awareness level training is intended for all relevant staff with responsibilities concerning the carriage of dangerous goods by passengers, as cargo, within mail or as stores and the staff of operators performing any of the following functions:

- **Ground handling**
- **Storage and loading of cargo**
- **Storage and loading of baggage**
- **Passenger handling and security staff responsible for screening passengers and their baggage**

The course has been accepted by the **Helideck Certification Agency (HCA)** and applies to offshore based personnel involved in helicopter operations. Training is in accordance with Part 1, Chapter 1.4 of the ICAO Technical Instructions and Section 1.5 of the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulation, Categories 7, 8 and 9.
